# Example: call follow-ups

Everything in this example is made up.

## What was pasted

Auto-transcript from Zoom, Tuesday, Oct 13. I'm Gwen Halloran at Hollis Creek Cabinetry. Tomas Reyes and Beth Okafor are from Northgate Property Group. Send the follow-up to both of them.

Gwen: So we're good on the natural oak for all twelve units?
Tomas: Yes, the, um, the natural oak. Beth, you're fine with that?
Beth: Fine by me. The budget cap is still 18,000 though.
Tomas: Okay. I'll send the revised quote with the, uh, oak pricing.
Gwen: And Tomas, can you send me the unit list? [crosstalk]
Tomas: Probably by Friday.
Beth: Who's pulling the permit for the lobby unit?
Gwen: Not sure yet. Maybe the building? We'll figure it out.
Gwen: I'd like to start the first week of November, once the deposit is in.
Tomas: Beth, can you handle the deposit?
Beth: Yeah, I'll look at it next week.

## The follow-up

**What was decided.**
- Natural oak finish for all twelve units. Tomas and Beth both agreed.

**Tasks.**
1. Tomas: send you the unit list. Friday, Oct 16 (he said "probably").
2. Beth: handle the deposit. Next week (she said "I'll look at it").
3. Send the revised quote with oak pricing. Owner unclear: the transcript labels that line Tomas, but quoting sounds like your job. No date given.

**Open questions.**
- Who pulls the permit for the lobby unit? You said "maybe the building" and it was left open.
- Does the revised quote stay under Beth's 18,000 budget cap? Nobody replied when she raised it.
- Start date: you suggested the first week of November once the deposit is in. Nobody confirmed.
- A stretch of crosstalk after you asked for the unit list was unreadable.
