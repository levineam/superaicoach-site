# Example: Monday plan

Everything in this example is made up.

## What was pasted

Calendar: Monday 9:00 to 10:00 supervisor meeting. Tuesday 2:00 to 3:00 walk-through at Kestrel Offices with Alma Torres. Wednesday dentist 11:00 to 1:00. Thursday is open. Friday I leave at noon for my sister's wedding.

I get about 4 hours a day for my own work, and about 2 on Friday. The rest is on-site with the crews.

To do:
- Proposal for Kestrel Offices, 2 hours. I told Alma she'd have it before the walk-through.
- Run payroll, 1 hour. Due Thursday at 5:00.
- Invoice October, 3 hours.
- Post the night supervisor ad and read 3 resumes, 1 hour.
- Call the insurance agent about the renewal, 30 minutes.
- Order floor cleaner and trash bags, 30 minutes. The closet is nearly empty.
- Fix the website pricing page, 3 hours.
- Finish the employee handbook, about 10 hours.
- Lowell Group wants me to sit through a 45 minute software demo. I said I'd get back to them.
- Van oil change.
- Start a monthly newsletter.
- 18 customer emails to answer.

I think Marisol, my office manager, can take the supplies order and the oil change.

Worries: nine invoices from September are still unpaid. Also Deon, my best supervisor, seems unhappy and I'm afraid he'll quit.

## The plan

**Can it all fit?** No. Your list adds up to about 21 hours of work, plus 18 emails. You have about 14 open hours. This plan uses about 9 and leaves room for things that run long.

**What matters most.**
1. Kestrel Offices proposal (2 hours). You told Alma she'd have it before Tuesday's walk-through.
2. Payroll (1 hour). Due Thursday at 5:00.
3. October invoices (3 hours). Nine September invoices are still unpaid, so money coming in matters this week.

**Day by day.**
- Monday (3 open hours): supervisor meeting at 9:00. Then 15 minutes with Deon (my suggestion, since you're worried he may quit). Then the Kestrel proposal, 2 hours. Send it today.
- Tuesday (3 open hours): answer the 18 emails, about 1 hour (a guess). Call the insurance agent, 30 minutes. Walk-through at 2:00.
- Wednesday (2 open hours): run payroll, 1 hour, a day early. Leave the rest loose.
- Thursday (4 open hours): October invoices, 3 hours. Then 30 minutes to remind the nine September accounts (my suggestion).
- Friday (2 hours before noon): post the night supervisor ad and read the 3 resumes, 1 hour.

**Delegate.**
- Marisol: the supplies order and the van oil change, as you suggested. Ask her Monday, since the closet is nearly empty.

**Drop or postpone.**
- Employee handbook (about 10 hours) and pricing page (3 hours): postpone. Neither has a deadline in your notes.
- Monthly newsletter: drop for now. Nothing else on your list depends on it.

**Say no to.**
- Lowell Group's software demo. You could say: "Thanks for the offer. I'm not looking at new software right now. I'll call you if that changes."

Missing: any deadline for the handbook and pricing page, and when Lowell Group expects your answer.
