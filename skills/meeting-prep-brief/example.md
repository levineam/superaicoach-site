# Example: meeting prep brief

Everything in this example is made up.

## What was pasted

Invite: Thursday 10:00, "Q1 renewal check-in." Dana Ruiz and Marcus Bell from Riverside Tile, plus me.

Dana, Oct 3: "Shipments to our Camden site were late twice in September. We need to talk about that before we renew."

Me, Oct 4: "Understood. We've added a second driver on Tuesdays. Happy to walk you through it."

Marcus, Oct 15: "Invoice 2214 still shows as open on our side. Can you confirm the credit for the September delays was applied?"

My note: I want them to sign the 12-month renewal. Our price goes up 4% in January.

## The brief

**The meeting.** Thursday at 10:00 with Dana Ruiz and Marcus Bell from Riverside Tile. They are deciding whether to renew.

**Who's coming.**
- Dana Ruiz, operations. Cares about on-time deliveries to the Camden site.
- Marcus Bell, finance. Wants invoice 2214 settled and the September credit confirmed.

**Where things stand.**
- Two late shipments to Camden in September (Dana, Oct 3).
- You added a second Tuesday driver (your reply, Oct 4).
- Invoice 2214 shows open on their side. Marcus asked on Oct 15 whether the September credit was applied, and there's no reply in the thread.

**What they probably want.**
- Proof the delivery fix is working. (Guess: October on-time numbers would help.)
- A clear answer on the credit before they talk about renewing.

**What you want.** A signed 12-month renewal. Your price goes up 4% in January.

**Questions to ask.**
1. How have Camden deliveries looked since the second driver started?
2. Is anything else about September still bothering you?
3. What would you need to see to sign for another 12 months?
4. Who else signs off on the renewal?

**Watch out for.**
- Marcus's Oct 15 email has no reply. Check whether the credit on invoice 2214 was applied before you walk in.
- The 4% increase comes right after a rough September. Decide how you'll bring it up.

Missing: October delivery numbers for Camden.
